Chat lives in the session

There is no separate chat box here. During a session (phase 2), you talk and type right inside the session window — the guest can mute their mic at any time, and their camera stays off unless they turn it on. Messages about a booking still live on its ticket, as always.

The toolbox — phase 2

Portable repair, diagnostic, and cleanup tools, kept on our side of the system. During a session the associate sends one into the guest’s device and runs it there — nothing installs, and no program is left behind when the session ends. The guest watches it happen. Each tool joins only after it is checked on its own maker’s site (the list and the checks are in the build report). Guest antivirus may ask about a portable tool the first time — that is expected, and the guest always sees it first.

On paper for phase 2: hardware and drive health checks · memory testing · driver cleanup · junk and startup cleanup · malware scanning · disk-space and system-file tools. Nothing is downloaded or run from this panel in phase 1.

How this console is set up

Our own relay (phase 2): remote.austinstechrepairgroup.com — sessions run on our system only, never a public service.
Session codes: 6 numbers, one use, good for 4 hours.
Member keys: made from the member’s own account, never stored, work only while the membership is active.
Screen-sharing waiver in use: v1-draft-2026-11-10 (draft wording — the owner’s approval is pending).
These are facts about the setup, shown here so any associate can see them; changing them is a build change, not a panel setting.

New Service Request — Triage

Walk-in and phone check-ins. Find the guest (or add them) — email and service address are required, because we come to them. For a repair, pick the device and the reason and run the power test. For a one-on-one lesson, tap One-on-One in the Service tiles, pick 1, 2, or 3 hours, and write what they want to get done. Either way the ticket lands straight in the Service Queue. Confirm the money with the guest before submitting — repairs: $15 visit fee for a known repair, $35 if the job needs diagnostics. One-on-one: $25 an hour for the time booked, and the $15 visit fee charged separately. ATR One members pay no visit fee.

Price Book

Updated Nov 6, 2026

How pricing works

  • Repair prices are part cost plus flat labor — $50 for standard repairs
  • $100 for soldering and folding devices
  • Service work that is not a repair is billed at $25/hr
  • The visit fee is added once per job — $15 for a known repair, $35 when the job needs diagnostics — ATR One members pay no visit fee on covered devices
  • Member tier discounts apply to the repair price
  • Every line carries a standard price — part cost plus flat labor — so you can quote straight from the book
