About Austin's Tech Repair Group

Built to bring enterprise-grade service directly to your door.

Verified & Background-Checked Professional

Your safety, security, and privacy are foundational. Technicians are rigorously background checked, screened, and vetted, ensuring total peace of mind when welcoming a mobile technician into your home, office, or workspace.

Meet Austin.

Austin Tyler Caudill founded Austin's Tech Repair Group to completely redefine consumer electronics repair in Ohio. Moving away from impersonal storefronts and delayed turnaround times, Austin built a fully mobile operation grounded in absolute trust, transparent communication, and peerless craftsmanship.

Operating as a registered sole proprietorship under Austin Tyler Caudill, every single service call is handled personally by Austin. When you book a repair, you aren't handed off to a third-party subcontractor or a junior tech—you get direct accountability, deep technical acumen, and meticulous attention to detail from the founder himself.

Beyond surface-level swaps. True component engineering.

Most commercial repair shops treat electronics as modular blocks, throwing away entire logic boards or assemblies for minor failures. We operate at the absolute micro-level.

Equipped with laboratory-grade optical microscopes, precision hot air rework stations, and advanced digital multimeters, we diagnose and resolve complex faults down to individual surface-mount components (SMD). Whether it's mapping a shorted power rail on a liquid-damaged smartphone motherboard, replacing blown integrated circuits on a game console, or recovering data from a dead computer logic board, we bring dead hardware back to life with dealership-level precision.

Parts Policy & Ordering Terms

All necessary replacement parts are paid for by the customer upon a spoken or written agreement to move forward with the repair. We collect payment for the parts and then order them on your behalf, or the customer can pay for the part directly. Unless otherwise stated, we choose the parts supplier.

Customer-Supplied Parts: While customers are welcome to supply components like hard drives and RAM, we do not accept displays or small internal components unless sourced through our trusted supplier network.
