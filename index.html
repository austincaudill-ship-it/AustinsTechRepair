1-Year Limited Warranty Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

Austin's Tech Repair Group ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") proudly stands by the quality of our component-level diagnostics, technical repairs, and replacement parts. We offer a 1-Year Limited Warranty on qualifying repairs subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions outlined below.

1. Scope of Coverage

This 1-Year Limited Warranty covers:

  • Replacement parts installed by Austin's Tech Repair Group (such as screens, batteries, charging ports, and modular components) against manufacturer defects in materials and workmanship.
  • The specific labor and technical craftsmanship performed by our technicians for the authorized repair.

2. Warranty Period

  • The coverage period begins on the date the repaired device is picked up or shipped back to the Customer ("Customer," "you").
  • This warranty remains in effect for exactly three hundred sixty-five (365) days from the date of service completion.

3. Exclusions & Void Conditions

This 1-Year Limited Warranty does NOT cover, and shall be rendered immediately null and void in the event of:

  • Physical Damage: Subsequent drops, impacts, pressure cracks, bent frames, or structural damage occurring after the repair.
  • Liquid / Moisture Damage: Any exposure to water, humidity, steam, or other liquids following the service.
  • Customer or Third-Party Tampering: Devices that have been opened, modified, serviced, or repaired by any party other than Austin's Tech Repair Group.
  • Software Issues: Malware, viruses, operating system corruption, software glitches, or issues resulting from user modifications (such as rooting or jailbreaking).
  • Pre-Existing Conditions: Failures or defects unrelated to the specific parts replaced or services performed during the original repair invoice.
  • Normal Wear and Tear: Gradual battery capacity degradation over time or minor cosmetic scratches that do not impede device functionality.

4. Warranty Claim Process

  • To file a claim under this 1-Year Limited Warranty, you must present the original repair invoice or receipt.
  • The device must be brought to our attention for inspection. Our technicians will test and evaluate the claimed defect to determine eligibility.
  • If a covered defect is found, Austin's Tech Repair Group will, at our discretion, either repair the defective part or replace it with an equivalent component at no additional charge for parts or labor.

5. Limitation of Liability

  • This warranty is limited strictly to the repair or replacement of the defective part or service performed.
  • Austin's Tech Repair Group is not liable for any incidental, consequential, or indirect damages, including loss of data, loss of business, or inability to use the device during the warranty evaluation or repair period.
