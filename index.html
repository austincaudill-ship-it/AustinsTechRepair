About Us

Built to keep tech simple.

Meet Austin.

Austin Tyler Caudill founded Austin's Tech Repair Group to bring a new standard of precision to consumer electronics repair in Ohio. With a deep technical foundation and years of corporate management experience leading teams, Austin built this business on trust, transparency, and doing the job right the first time.

As the sole proprietor, when you hand over your device, you know exactly who is fixing it.

Beyond the screen replacement.

Most repair shops stop at the surface. We go deeper. We provide true component-level diagnostics.

Whether it requires intricate micro-soldering, hot air rework, or mapping out a shorted logic board with a digital multimeter, we have the tools and the expertise to bring dead devices back to life. Dealership-level quality, without the dealership prices.
