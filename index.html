Services

Phones

Phones are the device most people live on, so a phone repair is about getting you back to normal without losing your photos, your apps, or your day. We come to you, diagnose the phone in person, and tell you plainly what is wrong before any part is ordered. Most phone work is screens, batteries, and charging ports, but we also handle cameras, speakers, buttons, back glass, and board-level faults that a screen swap will not fix.

What we do

  • Cracked and dead screens, including touch and display problems
  • Batteries that drain fast, shut off early, or swell
  • Charging ports that only work at an angle or not at all
  • Cameras, speakers, microphones, and buttons
  • Back glass and water damage checks
  • Board-level diagnosis when the fault is deeper than a part

Why we should fix it

A phone is glued together, with thin cables and a battery that has to be handled right. A rushed home repair can tear a cable, damage a connector, or leave a phone that looks fixed but loses touch or Face ID calibration. We test the screen, touch, charging, camera, and speakers before we leave, and the repair carries a 1-year warranty on parts we supply and on our labor.

Services

Tablets

Tablets get used hard at home, at school, and at work, and they break in a different way than phones. The glass is bigger, the adhesive holds more, and a tablet screen job takes real time, about 3 hours for a tablet screen. We tape cracked glass first so it does not spread, check whether the tablet powers on and is backed up, and confirm the fault before ordering anything.

What we do

  • Cracked glass and digitizer replacement
  • Display replacement and touch problems
  • Batteries and charging port repair
  • Buttons and speakers
  • Diagnosis when a tablet will not power on or charge

Why we should fix it

Big glass and strong adhesive are where home tablet repairs go wrong. Prying a tablet open without heat and patience cracks the display underneath, and a tablet that is not tested after the repair can leave you with dead touch in one corner. We take the time the job actually needs, test touch across the whole screen, and make sure it charges before we leave.

Computers

Computers are where a slow machine, a dead screen, or a lost file turns into a work problem. We fix laptops and desktops of all kinds, at your place. We start by finding whether the fault is hardware, software, or both, because replacing a part will not fix a software problem, and reinstalling software will not fix a failing drive.

What we do

  • Laptop and desktop screen replacement
  • SSD upgrades and RAM installs for slow machines
  • Battery and keyboard replacement
  • Virus cleanup and software problems
  • Data rescue when a drive is failing or a machine will not boot
  • Board-level work when the fault is on the board itself

Game consoles fail in ways that look like a software problem but are usually hardware: overheating, a disc drive that will not read, or an HDMI port that has been knocked loose. What we do

  • Disc drive repair and replacement
  • Cooling fans, cleaning, and overheating fixes
  • Storage upgrades
  • Controller repair
  • Board-level diagnosis for consoles that will not power on

Why we should fix it

Consoles are packed tight, with small connectors and heat that builds up fast.
