About Us

Built to keep tech simple.

Austin's Tech Repair Group delivers practical repairs, transparent communication, and rapid support for residential and business clients across the Miami Valley and Southeastern Ohio.

Since 2010, we have specialized in hardware support for phones, tablets, computers, gaming consoles, and smartwatches, alongside comprehensive B2B and managed IT services. As an independent provider, we maintain flexibility while utilizing premium parts backed by a solid 1-year warranty.

We bring expert service directly to you. From cracked screens and dead laptops to complex office network deployments, our team provides fast turnaround and clear communication throughout every stage of the repair process.
