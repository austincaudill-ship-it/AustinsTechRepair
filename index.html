Our values

What this business stands on

Austin's Tech Repair Group has run on the same values since the first phone was fixed in 2010. They decide how we quote, how we repair, and how we treat the people — and the community — who trust us with their devices.

Trust

Earned on every repair

People hand us the devices their lives run on — family photos, work files, the phone a family business takes its calls on. That trust has been earned one repair at a time since 2010, back when the bench was a desk in a bedroom and the customers were classmates.

Every job runs on paper the customer can keep: a written estimate first, a signed repair authorization before work begins, and a receipt with the customer's own signature when the job is done. Repairs are backed by a 1-year warranty on parts and labor, and the whole file stays with the customer's record, ready to pull up any time.

Transparency

The same numbers, on the table

A repair price should be explainable in one breath. Estimates come as flat, itemized numbers before any work starts — parts, labor, and the visit fee in the open — so the price agreed at the door is the price on the receipt.

When a part needs ordering, the customer receives the order number and the tracking link, and both are filed with their repair. The quote is built from the same supplier figures we see ourselves: one set of numbers, shared with the person paying the bill.

Environment

Repair is the original recycling

Every device repaired is a device kept in service — and one fewer device in a landfill, one fewer new device manufactured to replace it. Fixing what already exists is the most practical environmental choice there is, and it is the whole point of a repair shop.

Parts removed during a repair come back with us and are recycled through proper channels, so batteries and circuit boards stay out of the household trash. Some original parts return to their manufacturer under core and return programs. Either way, every take-off part is accounted for and handled responsibly.

Education

Skills grow when somebody shares them

This business started with Austin teaching himself iPhone repair — by 2010 he was fixing phones for fellow students, learning the trade the hands-on way, one opened device at a time. In 2013 he went to Diamond Oaks here in Ohio to perfect it, and that is where he learned to solder.

That is why we champion students learning to code and to repair. The next technician, programmer, or shop owner is sitting in a classroom today, and a skill picked up young can turn into a livelihood. A skill picked up young can turn into a livelihood — that is exactly what happened here.

Right to repair

Your device. Your choice.

Buying a device means owning it — the whole thing, inside and out. The freedom to open it, repair it, and choose who does the work is part of what ownership means, and independent repair shops keep that freedom real for everyday owners.

We support the right to repair: parts, manuals, and diagnostic information available to owners and independent shops alike. When people can fix what they own, devices last longer, local skills run deeper, and money stays in the owner's pocket. Your device — your choice of who fixes it.

Small business

Small business, standing with small business

Austin's Tech Repair Group is a small business in the truest sense: owner-run, with the same person answering the phone, giving the estimate, and doing the repair. We know exactly what a dead shop computer, a silent register, or a down network costs, because a business this size feels every hour of it.

So we keep local businesses running: commercial IT support, network installs, and websites built for the shops, crews, and offices around us. Doing business locally keeps the money, the skills, and the trust circulating right here in Spring Valley and across the Miami Valley.

That flag hung over the first parts wall. It still stands for how this business runs: proudly American owned and operated in the United States. No outsourcing.

We support the American dream for all.
