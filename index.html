1-Year Limited Warranty & Claims

Last updated October 20, 2026

Austin's Tech Repair Group stands behind its work. Every standard repair includes a 1-Year Limited Warranty on the parts we install and the labor we perform, under the terms below.

1. What This Warranty Covers

  • Replacement parts we installed (screens, batteries, charging ports, and other components) that fail due to a defect in the part itself.
  • The labor and workmanship of the specific repair on your invoice.

2. Warranty Period

  • Coverage runs for one year (365 days) from the date the repair was completed.
  • The warranty applies to the original customer and the device listed on the repair invoice. Please keep your invoice or receipt — it is your proof of coverage.

3. What This Warranty Does Not Cover

  • New physical damage: Drops, impacts, cracks, pressure damage, or bent frames after the repair.
  • Liquid damage: Exposure to water, moisture, or steam after the repair.
  • Tampering: Devices opened, modified, or repaired by anyone other than Austin's Tech Repair Group after our service.
  • Software problems: Viruses, malware, operating-system corruption, or issues caused by rooting, jailbreaking, or other modifications.
  • Unrelated failures: Problems in parts or systems that were not part of the original repair, including pre-existing conditions.
  • Normal wear: Gradual battery capacity fade over time and minor cosmetic wear that does not affect function.

4. How to File a Warranty Claim

  • Step 1 — Contact us: Call or text (937) 280-6243 or email austin.caudill@austinstechrepairgroup.com and describe the problem.
  • Step 2 — Show your invoice: Present your original repair invoice or receipt so we can verify coverage.
  • Step 3 — Inspection: We inspect and test the device to confirm the failure is a covered defect in the part or our workmanship.
  • Step 4 — Remedy: If the claim is covered, we will repair or replace the defective part at no charge for parts or labor. If the part cannot be repaired or replaced, we will refund the price of that repair.
  • Warranty inspections are diagnostic visits; the standard $15 dispatch fee does not apply to a covered warranty claim.

5. Limits

  • This warranty is limited to the repair, replacement, or refund described above. We are not liable for incidental or consequential damages, including loss of data or loss of use of the device during evaluation or repair.
  • Some states do not allow limits on implied warranties or consequential damages, so parts of this section may not apply to you. This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may have other rights that vary by state.
