About Us

Built to keep tech simple.

Austin's Tech Repair Group is built around practical repairs, clear communication, and fast support for home and business customers across the Miami Valley and Southeastern Ohio.

Since 2010, we've handled phone, tablet, computer, gaming, and smart watch support, plus ongoing B2B and managed IT services. We're an independent repair provider — not affiliated with or endorsed by any manufacturer — but we use quality parts and back every repair with a 1-year warranty.

We bring the service to you. Whether it's a cracked screen, a dead laptop, or your entire office network, our team responds fast and communicates clearly from diagnosis to pickup.
